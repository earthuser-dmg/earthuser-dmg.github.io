01 / Context

A shared platform.
A fragmented experience.

Making sense of the business before designing the system.

I joined Decathlon as a Product Designer in 2022. My first project was the Business Module System (BMS), a design framework for an internal integration platform. The concept was new to stakeholders, design assets were incomplete, and I was learning a new industry.

The platform connected domain-specific tools rather than operating each business product itself. My scope included modules used in inventory management, demand forecasting and other supply-chain workflows.

A consistent way to work, across products and teams.

Foundations

Reusable grids, patterns, icons and components.

Workflows

Modules organized around recurring business tasks.

Collaboration

A request process and shared specifications.

Learning

UX practices introduced through product delivery.
